3. SEVERAL RANGES IN ONE POSTING, AND WHAT IS LEFT OUT

Rule: keep the valid ranges (not a placeholder, not suspect), prefer USD, then prefer a range that is
not on-target earnings, then take the highest yearly midpoint, ties going to the first listed. Employers
list pay zones from the highest-cost area down (Zone 1, Zone A, 'San Francisco, New York, Seattle'), so
the highest range is the top-zone band, the band a posting in those cities would quote. The posting's
own location cannot be matched to a zone for most of these postings, because the zones are defined on
the employer's website. multi_zone marks the postings where the pick was made among two or more
different USD base-pay ranges. The rule overstates pay for a posting in a lower-cost zone: the hand
checks found 4 such picks among the 80 (section 2, notes marked location).
